# PRODUCT.md — Briks Building Services

## Register
brand

Default register is **brand**: briks.au is a marketing site, and client-facing proposal and
estimate pages (`public/estimates/*`) are brand documents where the presentation itself builds
trust. Internal tools live in a separate app (ops.briks.au) and use the product register.

## Product purpose
Briks Building Services is an Adelaide property maintenance, repair and small-build coordinator.
One local business is the single point of contact: Briks sends trusted tradies, quotes, manages
the work and invoices, with before/after photos. The website wins work; estimate and proposal
pages help clients decide on a specific job and spend.

## Users
- **Primary (estimate/proposal pages): property managers, agencies and investors.** They
  compare options and quotes, often on a phone between other tasks. They want the numbers,
  what's included and what happens next, fast, without hunting.
- Homeowners and landlords (also common): need the same clarity, plus reassurance.
- Tradies joining the network (/join).

## Brand personality
Three words: **premium, local, precise.**
- Premium / modern luxury: brass, warm paper, refined type; a high-end trade business, not a
  cheap job board.
- Local and personal: Adelaide, one owner who answers the phone; plain Australian English.
- Precise and organised: itemised, documented, on time, no surprises. Numbers line up.

## Anti-references (never look like)
- Generic builder template: hi-vis yellow/orange, hard-hat stock photos, cheap tradie look.
- Techy SaaS dashboard: dark mode by default, neon accents, app widgets.
- Busy or salesy: banners, countdowns, pushy CTAs, exclamation marks.
- Spreadsheet-like: dense tables and tiny text everywhere.

## Strategic principles
1. Lead with the answer: options, price ranges and what's included before detail.
2. Scannable in 30 seconds on a phone; complete enough to forward to an owner or investor.
3. Every figure has a clear label and unit; ranges are honest and explained once.
4. Calm confidence: no hype words, no em dashes, no exclamation marks.
5. Internal costs, margins and supplier details never appear on client-facing pages.

## Accessibility
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for text; visible keyboard focus (brass ring); respect
prefers-reduced-motion; all controls at least 44 px touch targets; no information by colour alone.
