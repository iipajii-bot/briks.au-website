How it works in four steps

Your tenants call one number. We do the rest.

  1. Step 1

    Your property manager emails tenants once. Briks writes the email with the Briks number.

  2. Step 2

    Tenants contact Briks. We work out how urgent it is and tell them what happens next.

  3. Step 3

    We organise the licensed trade, book access, keep everyone updated and check the finished work.

  4. Step 4

    Your property manager sees the job done, with before and after photos. Anything over the approval limit is quoted first.

What changes for your property managers, and what stays yours

What changes

  • Tenant repair calls go to one number.
  • Your property managers stop booking trades and chasing updates.
  • A Friday report lists every job, with photos.

What stays yours

  • Your approval limit stands. Anything over it is quoted to you first.
  • Your owners stay with your agency.
  • Preferred trades can come on board. They then work through Briks.

How pricing works

Every job is priced on its own. Nothing is charged to the agency.

  • Cost to the agencyNone
  • Who pays for the workThe owner
  • Under the approval limitThe job goes ahead
  • Over the approval limitQuoted to you first
  • Requests that need no workNo charge

Our targets: emergency call-back within 15 minutes. Urgent jobs booked the same day. Routine jobs booked within 2 business days.
