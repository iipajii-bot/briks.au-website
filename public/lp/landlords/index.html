What happens when a tenant reports a repair

One number for the tenant. Briks handles the trade, the access and the updates.

  1. The tenant's report goes to Briks

    The tenant reports the repair to Briks. We work out how urgent it is.

  2. The licensed trade and the access

    We book a licensed trade for the work, book access and keep the tenant updated.

    Our targets: emergency call-back within 15 minutes. Urgent jobs booked the same day. Routine jobs booked within 2 business days.

  3. Your approval, then the photos

    Anything over your approval limit is quoted to you by text before work goes ahead. Every job comes back to you with photos and one invoice.

What you control. Your limit, your yes or no, every photo.

Each job is priced on its own. Nothing over your limit starts without your reply.

The tenant note

Give this short note to your tenant. It has the Briks number in it.

Hi, please report any repair at the house to Briks Building Services on 0449 517 367. They will arrange the right licensed trade, keep you updated, and check with me before anything over our approval limit goes ahead. Thanks.
