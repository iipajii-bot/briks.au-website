Studio granny flat fit-out

Estimate prepared 14 October 2026Valid for 30 days

We'll turn your 7.00 × 5.60 m studio (39.2 m²) into a self-contained granny flat: a new bathroom, an updated kitchen, a built-in robe and a split-system air conditioner. The model is drawn to scale. Turn it around to see the layout.

What we'll do

  • Bathroom: built in the far left corner with new walls and a cavity sliding door. It has a 900 × 900 mm shower with a glass screen, a toilet, a 600 mm vanity with a mirror cabinet and an exhaust fan. The floor and shower are fully waterproofed and tiled.
  • Kitchen: a new IKEA L-shaped kitchen with drawers and overhead cupboards, a laminate benchtop, an inset sink and mixer, a 60 cm induction cooktop with an oven under it, and a rangehood. There's space for a fridge up to 700 mm wide.
  • Built-in robe: IKEA PAX, 2.0 m wide and 2.36 m high, with sliding doors and hanging and shelf space.
  • Air conditioning: we remove the old wall unit and patch the wall, then install a 2.5 kW reverse-cycle split system that heats and cools.
  • Plumbing and electrical: new hot, cold and waste lines to the bathroom and a new toilet connection to the sewer. New circuits for the cooktop, oven and air conditioner, plus bathroom lighting and fan, LED downlights, power points and a smoke alarm. All work is done by licensed trades with compliance certificates.
  • Finish: patch and paint the walls, then clean up and take all rubbish away.
